# Prior art

- `mr-js/fallout_quests` demonstrates demand for an offline reference, while its
  mirrored-HTML/Fandom approach and large transfer warning reinforce OVERSEER's
  targeted structured-data design.
- `shelbyspeegle/Fallout-Terminal` and `joshdentremont/fallout-terminal` explore
  terminal presentation and hacking simulation; OVERSEER does not reproduce
  that interaction model.
- `Wuerschtla/Fallout_Terminal` recreates a specific in-world terminal with
  authored entries; OVERSEER instead models cross-work evidence and provenance.

No code, data, or assets were copied from these projects. Their licenses were
therefore not imported into this repository.
